## Summary
Cross-framework synthesis of how DAMA-DMBOK, EDM Council DCAM, Gartner, and Forrester each define data standards and their taxonomies. All four converge on semantic, structural, quality, security, and interoperability dimensions — though each frames it differently. The 5-dimension taxonomy is an industry synthesis, not a single published framework.

## Key Claims
- **DAMA-DMBOK**: Standards are "fundamental rules and specifications governing how data is structured, defined, and managed throughout its lifecycle" — treats data as shared enterprise asset.
- **EDM Council DCAM**: Standards are "blueprints for data meaning and structure" — essential for analytics, cloud strategy, and risk management.
- **Gartner**: Standards are "specifications widely accepted by users... critical to compatibility." Defines data standards as a "universal language ensuring accuracy, interoperability, and quality." Notes 60% of AI projects will fail by 2026 due to data quality gaps.
- **Forrester**: Standards as prerequisites for "immediate, connected data experiences" and data products. Emphasizes taxonomy and metadata standards to reduce technical debt.
- Framework evidence table confirms all four address: Semantic, Structural, Quality, Security, Integration dimensions — with different emphasis.
- DMBOK aligns Semantic to "Metadata Management" and "Data Modeling"; Forrester calls it the "semantic layer."
- DMBOK aligns Structural to "Data Architecture"; Gartner identifies it as essential for data fabrics.
