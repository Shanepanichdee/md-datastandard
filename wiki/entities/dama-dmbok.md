## Definition
The Data Management Body of Knowledge (DAMA-DMBOK) is a comprehensive, vendor-neutral framework organizing data management into 11 core knowledge areas arranged in the "DAMA Wheel," with Data Governance at the center. Published by DAMA International, it is the most widely referenced framework for treating data as a strategic enterprise asset.

## Key Attributes
- **Structure**: 11 knowledge areas — Data Governance, Data Architecture, Data Modeling & Design, Data Storage & Operations, Data Security, Data Integration & Interoperability, Document & Content Management, Reference & Master Data, Data Warehousing & BI, Metadata Management, Data Quality.
- **Implementation approach**: Phased roadmap — begin with capability maturity assessment, prioritize 2–3 high-impact areas (commonly Data Quality or Metadata Management), then expand.
- **Context diagrams**: Outputs of one knowledge area serve as inputs to another — governance cannot be treated in isolation.
- **Stewardship integration**: Defines roles for data owners, stewards, and custodians explicitly within the governance knowledge area.
- **Not prescriptive**: DMBOK describes what to do, not how — organizations adapt it to their culture and technology stack.
