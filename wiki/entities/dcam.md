## Definition
The Data Management Capability Assessment Model (DCAM), developed by the EDM Council, is an assessment-driven framework for establishing and sustaining data management capabilities. It uses a structured maturity scale to benchmark organizational capabilities and drive continuous improvement — most widely adopted in financial services and regulated industries.

## Key Attributes
- **Version**: DCAM v3 (announced 2025).
- **Maturity scale**: 1 (Not Initiated) → 6 (Enhanced) — scored per capability, not as a single org-level number.
- **Three component groups**:
  - *Foundation*: Strategy & Funding — the "why" and resource commitment.
  - *Execution*: Architecture, Quality, Governance — the operational layer.
  - *Collaboration*: Data Control Environment — cross-functional accountability and audit.
- **Use in practice**: Organizations score capabilities, identify functional gaps, track lineage and policy enforcement, and align funding models to improvement roadmaps.
- **Semantic focus**: Emphasizes data meaning and structural consistency as prerequisites for advanced analytics, cloud strategies, and risk management.
